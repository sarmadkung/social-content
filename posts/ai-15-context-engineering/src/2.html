AI Engineering #15

Four questions per step

1
Is it relevant to this step?
Not to the whole session. Only to what the model must do right now.
let it in
2
Can it be summarised?
Example:twelve tool calls become three lines of outcome.
shrink it
3
Can it be fetched when needed?
Example:needed once in twenty steps? Make it a tool call, not a prompt section.
load it later
4
Is it stale?
Example:an old plan or a replaced file competes with the current truth, and sometimes wins.
remove it
It is closer to cache design than prompt writing: what enters, what gets evicted, what is out of date.
