list.includes()
checks the list one item at a time
100,000 orders × 100,000 IDs = up to 10 billion checks
Set.has()
about one step per lookup (average)
100,000 lookups + 100,000 to build the Set
Big-O is how the work grows as the input grows. Here the list check grows with orders × IDs; the Set check grows with orders + IDs.
