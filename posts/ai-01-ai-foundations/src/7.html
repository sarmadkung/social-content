Three ways a model can learn

Supervised
learns fromexamples with answers
It learns from examples that come with the right answer, and copies the pattern.
Example: 10,000 emails, each one marked "spam" or "not spam". Our spam filter and house-price model learn this way.
Unsupervised
learns fromdata with no answers
Nobody gives it answers. It finds groups and patterns in the data by itself.
Example: a shop's model sorts customers into groups that buy similar things, without being told what the groups are.
Reinforcement
learns fromrewards
It tries actions and gets a reward when they go well, so it does more of what works.
Example: a game player that gets points for winning, and slowly learns which moves win.
