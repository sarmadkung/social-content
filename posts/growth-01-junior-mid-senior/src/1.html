Dev Growth #01

Seniority grows by scope, not years

Junior
owns a task
  • Gets a clear ticket and asks for help.
  • Learns the codebase.
  • Done = working code that passes review.
Mid-level
owns a feature
  • Turns a vague request into tasks.
  • Finishes without much help.
  • Spots edge cases (rare inputs that break things, like an empty name).
Senior
owns a problem
  • Asks "should we build this at all?"
  • Sees risks early.
  • Makes the whole team faster.
Scope = how much you are trusted to own, with no one checking on you
