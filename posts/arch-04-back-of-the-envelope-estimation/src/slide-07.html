What the numbers decide

200 : 1
views per upload
→ Read-heavy. Make reading fast first.
730 TB / yr
photo files
→ Keep them in object storage, not the database.
Object storage: a service that stores files by name. Example: Amazon S3.
365 GB / yr
metadata
→ One ordinary database holds it for a long time.
30 / s
uploads at peak
→ Writes are modest. No sharding yet.
Sharding: splitting one database across many machines.
→ Pick tools after the maths. "We need sharding" means nothing until you know writes are about 30 a second.
