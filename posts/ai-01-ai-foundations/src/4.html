Three kinds of answer

Same idea every time: numbers in, maths, answer out. What changes is the kind of answer.
A category
classification
Picks one answer from a fixed list.
Examplea new email
→
"spam" or "not spam"
A number
regression
Gives any value on a scale.
Examplea house with 3 rooms
→
300 (thousand dollars)
New content
generative AI
Makes new text, images or audio that did not exist before. LLMs are generative.
Example"reply to this email"
→
a written reply
