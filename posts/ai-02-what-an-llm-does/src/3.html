AI Engineering #02

One loop explains three surprises

01
It can sound sure and still be wrong
why: it picks likely text, not checked facts
Example: ask for a book's page count. You get a number that sounds right, but nothing checked it.
02
It forgets you between calls
why: the model keeps no memory; your app resends the chat
Example: tell it your name, then open a new chat. Unless the app saved it, the model doesn't know it.
03
Long answers cost more and take longer
why: each token is one more trip around the loop
Example: a 300-token reply takes 300 predictions. A 30-token reply takes 30.
