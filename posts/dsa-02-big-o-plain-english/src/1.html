Big-O counts
how work grows

Big-O: how an algorithm's work grows as n, the size of its input, grows. Example: delivering flyers on a street of n houses.
Big-Othe job6 → 12 houses
O(1)
One flyer to one house.
The street's size doesn't matter.
1 → 1 flyer
O(n)
One flyer to every house.
Twice the houses, twice the work.
6 → 12 flyers
O(n²)
Every house sends one to every other house.
Twice the houses, about 4× the work.
6×5 = 30
12×11 = 132
