Chunking is a ceiling

If the answer is split across two chunks, no retriever downstream can recover it.

Embeddings have a shape

A model trained on sentences will not match a one-word query. Test on your real questions.

Rerank earns the precision

Retrieve wide and cheap, then rerank narrow. Skipping this is why top-k feels random.
