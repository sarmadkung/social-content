Five minutes of maths before any design.

A photo app with 10 million daily users. How big is it? The slides size it step by step: assumptions, requests per day, requests per second, the evening peak, and storage per year.

The answer: 6,000 views a second at peak and 730 TB of photos a year, but only 30 uploads a second. Reads and storage are the hard part. Sharding can wait.
