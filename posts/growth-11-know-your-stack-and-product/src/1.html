Dev Growth #11

Pick a stack you could review

agent asks: "Store sessions in Redis or Postgres?"
A STACK YOU DON'T KNOW
you can't judge either option, so you guess
↓
the agent builds login, logout and rate limits on it
↓
weeks later: it doesn't fit how you deploy. Rewrite auth.
A STACK YOU KNOW
you know the trade-offs, so you choose
↓
"Postgres. We already run it."
↓
the agent builds on a choice you can defend
WHAT TO DO
Pick tools you could review a diff in. A boring stack you know beats a new one you don't.
Learn new tools on side projects, not on the product you need to ship.
