What do you still need to know?

familiarity is not the reason to pick a stack. you still have to own what ships:
Read itreview the agent's diff; you can't approve Rust you can't read (more in DEV GROWTH #10)
Measure itrun a profiler and a benchmark on data as big as production, to see where the time goes
Run itdeploy it, monitor it, debug it when it fails at 2 a.m.
Know what sits under every languagememory, threads, disk and network I/O, and how data sits in memory: these carry over to every language (more in DEV GROWTH #07)
ON TOP OF THATpick what the job needs, not the stack you're used to
