Years of experience don't make someone senior.
Five years of the same work is one year, five times.

The levels aren't about how much code you know. They're about how big a problem you can own without someone checking on you.

If you think seniority means knowing more frameworks, you'll learn frameworks and stay stuck.

The two images show:
1. What changes at each level
2. The same bug, fixed three ways

Seniority also shows in the work you prevent. "We don't need a new service, a database index is enough" can save weeks. Write those decisions down. Nobody sees work that didn't happen.

Junior owns a task. Mid owns a feature. Senior owns the outcome.
